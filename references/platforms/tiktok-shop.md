# TikTok Shop profile

## Scope and listing model

- This snapshot is for TikTok Shop US. Platform rules, Seller Center fields and category approvals vary by country and category; ask for both before drafting.
- Listing package fields: title, description, brand, category, required disclosures, product attributes, variations and image assets. Pull required attribute names and permitted values from the current Seller Center category flow.
- Category approval, restricted products, safety labels and legal disclosures cannot be inferred from product images alone.

## Content and image plan

- Use a concise title that identifies brand (when authorized), product type and distinguishing details. Do not list all variants or inventory, make promotional promises, or use clickbait.
- The current US listing guide describes 25–200 characters; a quality-tier guide separately describes 25–255. This disagreement is intentionally not treated as a universal hard title limit: follow the current Seller Center validator and preserve the source in the export.
- US image snapshot: up to 9 square images; at least 600×600 px; up to 10 MB each; JPG/JPEG/PNG; one main image selected. The main image must show a front physical view on pure white. Listing images must accurately show what the buyer receives; the current policy disallows placeholders and digital renderings. Added text, logos, borders, watermarks and graphics are prohibited across listing images, not only on the main image. Use actual product photos for distinct angles, detail, included contents, usage and size comparisons; avoid repeated angles.
- TikTok Shop distinguishes listing requirements from quality recommendations (for example, 5+ images and title quality). Keep these labels separate in copy and QA.
- Suggested photo coverage: primary/front, back/side, detail, included contents, dimensions/fit, in-use and category-required proof. Slots are selected according to product and category, not blindly filled. For this market, express benefits through accurate photography and the listing fields; do not place marketing copy or designed callouts over listing images.

## Preflight

1. Verify country, category, seller permissions, prohibited/restricted status and current category-specific documents.
2. Match product title, variation/SKU, images and package contents.
3. Check the live editor for title and upload limits; run visual checks for primary-image content and policy-sensitive claims.
4. Check disclosure and warning requirements for the product type and destination.

Sources and review dates are recorded in [`platform-rules.json`](platform-rules.json).
